A personalized starting point

You don’t need to have it all figured out

You don’t need a diagnosis or a perfectly clear goal to ask about therapy. We can begin with what has been difficult, how it is affecting you, and what you would like to feel different.

During an initial consultation, we’ll discuss whether my approach and the services I offer are a fit for your needs. If another type of care would be more appropriate, we can talk about that as well.

Appointments are available through telehealth, with in-person sessions in Hackensack by arrangement. We’ll confirm location and scheduling options during the consultation.
