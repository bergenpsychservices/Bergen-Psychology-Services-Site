Room to understand. Space to change.

When something isn’t working, understanding why is a place to start.

You may be overwhelmed by stress, carrying difficult experiences from the past, or finding yourself in patterns that are hard to change.

Sometimes the concern is clear. Other times, you simply know you’re not feeling or functioning the way you’d like.

Therapy offers a place to make sense of those experiences, feel heard, and begin trying new ways forward.

How I work

Thoughtful understanding.
Practical steps forward.

My approach draws primarily from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), adapted to your experiences and goals.
