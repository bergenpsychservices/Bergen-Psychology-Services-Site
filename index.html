How I Work

Many of my clients feel overwhelmed, stuck, or disconnected from the life they want to be living. My approach is collaborative and practical. I work with you to better understand what is keeping you stuck and what you would like to be different.

Depending on what brings you to therapy, our work may involve learning new ways to manage stress and difficult emotions, making sense of past experiences, changing patterns that are no longer working, or navigating challenges in relationships, work, and everyday life.

I draw primarily on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) to help you recognize unhelpful patterns, respond more flexibly to difficult thoughts and emotions, and take meaningful action toward your goals.

  • Cognitive Behavioral (CBT)
  • Acceptance & Commitment (ACT)
  • Prolonged Exposure Therapy
  • Cognitive Processing (CPT)
  • Dialectical Behavior (DBT)
  • Exposure Response Prevention (ERP)
  • Mindfulness-Based (MBCT)
  • Motivational Interviewing
  • Psychological Testing & Evaluation
  • Unified Protocol (UP)

About

Dr. Alan Sheinfil is a licensed clinical psychologist and professor at Fairleigh Dickinson University. He received his PhD from Syracuse University and completed a postdoctoral fellowship at the Michael E. DeBakey VA Medical Center. His work emphasizes evidence-based treatment for trauma, substance use, and anxiety.

He is licensed in New Jersey, New York, and Texas, and works with teens, adults, and elders in an individualized setting.
