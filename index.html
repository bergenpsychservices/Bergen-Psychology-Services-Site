Working Together

You might be dealing with more than feels doable right now. Reaching out to talk about what's been happening for you is a meaningful step.

I don't approach therapy as repairing something broken, but as making sense of the patterns that aren't serving you, and learning how to do things differently. My role is to be alongside you as we focus on understanding any obstacles you are experiencing, identifying the changes you'd really like to see, and helping your life feel more authentic.

In our sessions, we'll focus on exploring what feels hard to move through right now and what you're hoping will feel different over time. That might look like learning new ways to manage stress and difficult emotions, understanding how past experiences are affecting you now, changing habits or reactions that keep repeating and causing pain, or working through the tension or conflict that shows up with partners, family, colleagues, or in the day-to-day.

I draw primarily on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT). In practice, that means we'll work to notice habits of thinking and reacting that aren't serving you, build flexibility around intense or confusing emotions, and make changes that align with your values and priorities.
