Information We Collect

When you submit the consultation request form on this website, we collect the information you choose to provide: your name, email address, phone number, preferred contact time, and any message you include. This information is used solely to respond to your request and schedule a consultation.

How Your Information Is Used

Form submissions are received via a third-party form service (StaticForms) and delivered to our office email. Your information is not sold, shared, or used for marketing purposes. It is used only to contact you regarding your consultation request.

Data Storage & Retention

Submitted information is stored in our office email inbox and is not retained longer than necessary to respond to your inquiry. We do not store form submissions in any separate database or CRM system.

Health Information

Please do not include sensitive personal or health information in the website form. The form is not a secure, HIPAA-compliant channel. If you wish to discuss your health or treatment concerns, please do so during your consultation call or through a secure telehealth platform.

Your Rights
